          Every state, on purpose

          A reviewer should be able to see the states without editing code. The panel at the bottom right forces any screen into any state. “Focused” and “pressed” are produced by actually focusing and pressing the screen's main control, so they are the real thing rather than a picture of it.

          What each forced state is meant to show
          StateWhat it proves
          idleThe screen as it looks before anyone acts. No status is implied.
          focusedA visible focus ring on the primary control, using the real focus state.
          pressedThe pressed appearance, held long enough to look at.
          processingIndeterminate progress with the current stage named and a way to cancel.
          successAn outcome stated in words and an icon, not colour alone.
          failedWhat happened, what is preserved, and the next action.
          cancelledThat stopping was allowed, and what the stop did to the work.

          Grayscale check. Turn the screen grey with your operating system or a browser extension and walk the eight screens. Every status keeps its word and its icon, because colour is never the only cue. The three measured contrast pairs are listed in G1_G3_checks.md.
